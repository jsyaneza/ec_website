Research Interests

Focus and Academic Purpose

My academic motivation stems from a deep interest in understanding how people interact with their environment and how systemic decisions shape development opportunities. After twelve years of experience managing a business in the food sector, I experienced at a micro scale the consequences of public policies, market fluctuations, and regional productivity gaps.

My research objective is to use technical and econometric tools to analyze territorial dynamics, model regional interactions, and propose evidence-based public policy recommendations.

Main Lines of Interest

  • Regional Econometrics and Quantitative Economic Geography: Analysis of productivity gaps across departments and municipalities in Colombia.
  • Spatial Analysis and Transport Networks: Evaluation of the impact of geography, road infrastructure, and agglomeration effects on socioeconomic dynamics.
  • Quantitative Evaluation and Development Economics: Quantitative and intersectoral modeling of regional heterogeneities and territorial inequalities.
