Research Interests

Academic Focus and Purpose

My academic motivation stems from an interest in understanding how people interact with their environment and how systemic decisions shape development opportunities. Transcending the deterministic view of economic growth, I seek to understand the multidimensional nature of territorial development from an integrative perspective that recognizes the interconnections between spatial, economic, social, and environmental factors.

My research objective is to use technical and econometric tools to analyze territorial dynamics, model regional interactions, and propose evidence-based public policy recommendations that drive inclusive and sustainable development in Colombian regions.

Main Research Areas

  • Regional Econometrics and Quantitative Economic Geography: Analysis of productivity gaps between Colombian departments and municipalities.
  • Spatial Analysis and Transport Networks: Evaluation of the impact of geography, road infrastructure, and agglomeration effects on regional socioeconomic dynamics.
  • Quantitative Evaluation and Development Economics: Quantitative and intersectoral modeling of regional heterogeneities and territorial inequalities.

Projects

Analysis of the Impact of Road Infrastructure on Regional Development in Colombia

This project analyzes how terrestrial transportation networks and road infrastructure affect connectivity, transport costs, and productivity gaps between Colombian municipalities. The research integrates network analysis, spatial econometrics, and geomatics to decompose the mechanisms through which geography and infrastructure shape regional dynamics.

Summary: Using spatial econometric methodologies and quantitative geographic analysis, the research addresses regional disparities derived from geographic limitations, infrastructure quality, and network effects. By utilizing Open Street Map data and complex network analysis, the research develops indices of municipal accessibility and connectivity that allow for the evaluation of the differentiated impact of distinct typologies of road infrastructure on territorial economic development.
